What we built · one sentence

From teaching material → a full learning experience with pedagogy.

Reading · 2D H5 · 3D/VR · Quiz — staged the way a strong teacher prepares a lesson, not the way a slide generator dumps pages.

Aha keys→ Knowledge graph→ Outline→ Section fill
Problem

AI courses look complete. Whether they teach is another question.

Pretty content ≠ installed insight. Finishing a task with AI is not the same as learning from it.

Four connected failures

Why “PDF → course” usually sucks

01 · Learning

Gains are rare

Task success with AI ≠ durable learning once the tool is gone.

02 · Modality

Cheap ≠ right

Medium follows what is easy to generate, not what the concept needs.

03 · Coverage

No checkable goals

Without structure, units skip prerequisites and quiz untaught ideas.

04 · Guidance

Learners wander

No scaffold → practice → transfer path; no companion that knows where you are.

The litmus test we failed

Strip the visuals. Is any teaching strategy left?

Our early 3D spectacle demos looked impressive — then taught nothing. That moment reframed the project: generate teaching, not content piles.

Our bet

Pedagogy first. Content second.

Pedagogy Core→ Aha keys→ KG→ Outline→ Fill

Excellent teacher habits become data the system can check — not model goodwill.

Next: sample → live PDF → pipeline / skills / tools

Study · method

N = 6 · remote Zoom tryout

  • Pre-quiz → learn a sample course → post-quiz
  • NASA-TLX + satisfaction Likert + interview
  • Five subject packs (chem · bio · geo · mech · phys)

Gains reflect learner experience on pre-built samples — not live generation variance.

What participants named

Helped

Friction

Close · summary

Install keys. Don’t dump slides.

  • Pedagogy-first agent: Aha → KG → multimodal outline
  • 20 skills · 40 tools · planner / executor discipline
  • Study: large gains · high satisfaction · clear next fixes
