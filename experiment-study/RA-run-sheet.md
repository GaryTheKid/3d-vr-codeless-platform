## Steps (do in order)

1. **Say hi (1 min).**
   > "Thanks for helping test an AI teaching tool. There are no right/wrong answers — I just want your honest reaction. It'll take about 60–90 min, and I'll record the Zoom for our notes if that's OK."

2. **Assign a material pack.** Note the pack number/name in your table (e.g., “2 Chem-VSEPR”). Have that folder’s PDF + quiz ready.

3. **Pre-test.** Send the pack’s **quiz HTML** (e.g. `virus_quiz.html`). Ask them to:
   - Open it in the browser
   - Put their **participant code** (P0x) in the name field
   - Answer without looking anything up
   - Click **Download Answer Sheet (.txt)** and send the file back
   - Name / save your copy as `P0x_pre_answers.txt`
   - Do **not** show them the answer key or rubric

4. **Send the system link.** Ask them to open it in **Chrome or Edge on a computer** (not phone). Ask them to share their screen.

5. **Upload + build.** Send them (or have them use) the assigned **PDF only**. Tell them: upload the PDF → click **Build from this**.
   - **Write down the start time** (when they click Build).

6. **Wait for it to finish.** The course is done when **every section in the outline shows a check mark ✓**.
   - **Write down the finish time.** (start→finish = generation time)
   - While waiting, only help with "which button" — don't explain the topic.

7. **Start learning.** Tell them to click **Start Learning** and go through the whole course (reading, quizzes, interactives, 3D).
   - **Write down the learn start time.**

8. **Finish the course.** When they reach the end, ask: *"Did you open every section at least once?"*
   - **Write down the learn end time.** (start→end = learning time)

9. **Post-test.** Send the **same quiz HTML** again (fresh tab / re-open so answers aren’t still filled in). Same instructions as pre-test:
   - Participant code in the name field
   - Download `.txt` → send back
   - Save your copy as `P0x_post_answers.txt`

10. **Send the questionnaire.** Send `questionnaire.html`. Ask them to fill it, click **Download my answers**, and send the downloaded file back to you (email / chat). Save as `P0x_questionnaire.txt`.

11. **Short interview (5–10 min).** Ask the questions below. Jot notes or keep the recording.

12. **Wrap up (1 min).**
    > "That's it — thank you! The AI course can have mistakes; it's just for testing the tool."
    Stop recording. Save your notes as `P0x_notes`.

13. **After the call (you / grader).** Score pre and post with that pack’s `*_grading_rubric.html`. Record MCQ / short-answer / total (out of 30) for pre and post.

## Interview outline (semi-structured, 5–10 min)

Ask what you have time for. If they give a short answer, follow up with *"Can you give an example?"*

1. In one sentence, what does this tool do?
2. How did the **waiting / generation** feel? Too long, fine, fast?
3. Which part helped you learn most — reading, quizzes, interactive, or 3D? Why?
4. Where did you feel **confused or annoyed**?
5. Did the course **match the PDF** — too shallow, about right, too much?
6. Would you use this for **your own studying**? For **teaching someone else**?
7. If we could fix **only one thing**, what should it be?
8. Anything else you want to tell us?

*(Write a few quotes / notes under each — that's enough for the report.)*
