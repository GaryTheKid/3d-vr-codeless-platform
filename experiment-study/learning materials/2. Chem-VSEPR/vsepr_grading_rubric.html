Short Answer 1

Question: Explain the difference between electron-domain geometry and molecular geometry.

Expected content points:

  • Electron-domain geometry includes all bonding and nonbonding domains
  • Molecular geometry describes only the arrangement of atoms
  • Clearly contrasts the two terms

Expected content points:

  • VSEPR counts regions/domains rather than individual electron pairs in a multiple bond
  • Connects this to shape prediction around the central atom

Question: Explain how one or more lone pairs can make molecular geometry differ from electron-domain geometry.

Expected content points:

  • Lone pairs count as electron domains and repel bonding domains
  • They are included in electron-domain geometry but omitted from the molecular-geometry name
  • They can compress bond angles or change the visible atomic shape
