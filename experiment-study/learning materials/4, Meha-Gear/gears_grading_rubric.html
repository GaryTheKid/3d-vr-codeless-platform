Short Answer 1

Question: Explain how the number of teeth on driver and driven gears affects speed.

Expected content points:

  • Uses driven teeth ÷ driver teeth for the stated ratio convention
  • A larger driven gear turns more slowly than a smaller driver
  • A smaller driven gear turns faster than a larger driver

  • Bevel gear changes shaft direction, commonly by 90°
