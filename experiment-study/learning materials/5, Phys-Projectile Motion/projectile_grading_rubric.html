Short Answer 1

Question: Explain what it means to say that horizontal and vertical projectile motions are independent.

Expected content points:

  • Horizontal motion has zero acceleration and constant velocity
  • Vertical motion has acceleration -g
  • The two components can be analyzed separately and then combined
