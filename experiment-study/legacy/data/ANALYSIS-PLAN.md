# Analysis plan (course project — keep it easy)

Goal: enough numbers + a few quotes for your **class report**. No need for fancy stats.

## Spreadsheet

One row per person. Columns: see `DATA-DICTIONARY.md` (you can ignore half of them).  
Minimum useful columns: `pid, material_id, pretest_total, posttest_total, gain, tlx_raw, s1, gen_sec, learn_sec`, plus a notes cell.

## Quantitative (do this)

1. **Learning:** mean pre, mean post, mean `gain = post − pre`. A simple before/after bar chart is enough.  
2. **TLX:** average the 6 scores (use `100 − Performance` so higher = worse workload everywhere). Show a small table or bar chart.  
3. **Satisfaction:** mean of overall items S1–S3 (and optionally authoring / learning groups).  
4. **Time:** median generation minutes + median learning minutes.

Skip Wilcoxon / Cronbach / regression unless your course specifically asks for a p-value.

## Qualitative (do this)

1. Skim interview notes/recordings.  
2. Write **4–6 themes** in plain language, e.g. “waiting feels long”, “3D helped”, “don’t fully trust AI text”.  
3. Add **1 quote** under each theme (anonymous: P03).  
4. In the report, connect themes to one number (e.g. wait complaints ↔ high Temporal Demand).

## Report outline (≈2–3 pages)

1. What you built / what people did  
2. Who participated (N, rough background)  
3. Numbers (gain, TLX, satisfaction, timing)  
4. Interview themes  
5. What you’d improve next (3 bullets)  
6. Limitations in one short paragraph (small N, AI courses vary, Zoom fatigue)
